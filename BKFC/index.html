BKFC NHC Campaign Case

Project Leader work for the Bach Khoa Ambassador Club at Nguyen Huu Canh High School: direct career orientation, short-form content, and a reusable poster-caption library for TN THPT and ĐGNL exam milestones.

Case study

Not just a school visit.

The campaign needed to carry real value through every phase of consultation. I organized the message around student needs, admission milestones, and content formats that students would actually open.

What I did

Content, operations, and artifacts.

This was a compact campaign with visible outputs and data-informed content decisions.

Direct consultation

Structured career-orientation talking points for grade 12 students so the school visit could stay clear, useful, and repeatable across groups.

Content pivot

Used campaign performance signals to lean into short-form video when it proved stronger for reach and attention.

Poster system

Kept the exam-season content in a 12-piece poster archive with matching captions and upload dates.

Admission tool

Built a small lookup artifact to make admission guidance easier to present and revisit.

Review

Framed the results around views, non-follower reach, and format mix instead of vague “successful campaign” language.
